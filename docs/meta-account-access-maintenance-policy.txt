ACCOUNT ACCESS MAINTENANCE POLICY
=================================

Organization: Conexões Consultoria e Treinamentos Ltda
CNPJ: 45.659.882/0001-50
Application: Conexões Creators
Question reference: maintaining-16
Effective date: 30 October 2026

1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy defines how we grant, revoke, and review access for people in our
organization to systems that can affect Conexões Creators or Meta Platform Data
(e.g., Vercel, Supabase, GitHub, Meta Developer App roles, business email).

2. ANNUAL ACCESS REVIEW (HIGHLIGHTED)
-------------------------------------
>>> We review all access grants at least once every 12 months and revoke access
>>> that is no longer required. <<<

>>> We review all access grants at least once every 12 months and revoke access
>>> that is no longer being used. <<<

3. DEPARTURE / PROMPT REVOCATION (HIGHLIGHTED)
----------------------------------------------
>>> We promptly revoke all access grants when a person leaves the organization. <<<

Access is also revoked promptly when:
- Access is no longer required for the person's role; or
- Access is no longer being used.

4. PROCEDURE
------------
1) Maintain a list of people with elevated access to production systems.
2) At least annually, review each grant against current role and usage; remove unused
   or unnecessary access.
3) On departure or role change: same day when possible, remove Vercel/Supabase/GitHub/
   Meta app roles and rotate shared secrets if the person had access to them.

5. ATTESTATION
--------------
Conexões Consultoria e Treinamentos Ltda implements the account maintenance processes
above.
