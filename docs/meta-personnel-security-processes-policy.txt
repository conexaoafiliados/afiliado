PERSONNEL SECURITY PROCESSES — PLATFORM DATA ACCESS
===================================================

Organization: Conexões Consultoria e Treinamentos Ltda
CNPJ: 45.659.882/0001-50
Application: Conexões Creators
Question reference: securityprocesses-23
Effective date: 30 October 2026

1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy states security processes for people who have access to Meta Platform Data.

2. PROCESSES IN PLACE (HIGHLIGHTED)
-----------------------------------
>>> Before / while accessing Platform Data, personnel are subject to: <<<

- Confidentiality / acceptable use obligations (see meta-acceptable-use-platform-data.txt)
  before processing Platform Data on devices.
- Information security awareness as part of onboarding for new personnel with access
  (MFA policy, token/secret handling, log/review expectations).
- Regular ongoing awareness at least annually for people with continued access
  (refresh of AUP, MFA, and incident reporting expectations).
- Role-related guidance for operators who administer Vercel/Supabase/Meta app credentials.
- Return of company assets (e.g., laptop) and prompt access revocation upon separation
  from the organization (see meta-account-access-maintenance-policy.txt).

Background checks may be used when required by role/risk; at minimum, access is limited
to authorized operators of Conexões Consultoria e Treinamentos Ltda.

3. ATTESTATION
--------------
Conexões Consultoria e Treinamentos Ltda maintains personnel security processes for
people with access to Platform Data as described above.
