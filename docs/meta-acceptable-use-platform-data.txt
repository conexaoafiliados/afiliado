ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY — META PLATFORM DATA ON DEVICES
=====================================================

Organization: Conexões Consultoria e Treinamentos Ltda
CNPJ: 45.659.882/0001-50
Application: Conexões Creators
Effective date: 30 October 2026

1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy governs how people in our organization may process Meta Platform Data
on organizational or personal devices (e.g. laptops used for development).

2. DEFINITION
-------------
"Platform Data" means data received from Meta (including Meta/Instagram user IDs,
access tokens, app secrets, and related metrics synced via Meta APIs).

3. ALLOWED USE (HIGHLIGHTED)
----------------------------
>>> People in our organization may process Platform Data on devices ONLY when there
>>> is a clear and actionable business purpose (for example: developing, debugging,
>>> operating, or supporting Conexões Creators). <<<

4. PROHIBITED USE
-----------------
- Selling, licensing, or sharing Platform Data outside approved service providers
- Storing Platform Data in personal cloud drives (e.g. personal Dropbox/Google Drive)
  except as required for approved business tools
- Using Platform Data for unrelated personal projects

5. DELETION WHEN PURPOSE ENDS (HIGHLIGHTED)
-------------------------------------------
>>> When the business purpose no longer exists, Platform Data must be deleted from
>>> the device as soon as reasonably possible (for example: remove local .env copies
>>> with secrets that are no longer needed; clear local exports; revoke unused tokens). <<<

6. TECHNICAL SAFEGUARDS (RECOMMENDED)
------------------------------------
Where available, devices should use full-disk encryption (e.g. BitLocker on Windows
or FileVault on macOS), screen lock, and up-to-date OS security patches.

7. ATTESTATION
--------------
Members of Conexões Consultoria e Treinamentos Ltda who handle Conexões Creators
are obligated to follow this Acceptable Use Policy.
